This is the blog where we build Galascribe in public. Galascribe turns a creator's videos and writing into articles on a website the creator owns: Markdown files in a GitHub repository under their own account, published to GitHub Pages, with every article linking back to its source.

Anand, the founder, writes it. Two posts a week, Sunday and Friday: how the product works, the decisions behind it, and the incidents and reversals along the way, with dates and numbers rather than adjectives. The drafts live in this blog's own repository as files, and each one carries the date it goes out.

## How we write here

- Say what we did, when, and what it cost; name the mistakes.
- Keep every figure traceable to a record we can point at.
- Never name a creator we have written to without their say.
- Revise in public when the facts change, and say so in the post.

The site is created and published with Galascribe. Its Markdown content and history belong to the repository, not to us.
